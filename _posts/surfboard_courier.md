## *COURIER: The End of the Line*

Every surfboard has a life before it ever touches water. Someone wraps it, straps it into a van full of other boards, and drives it wherever it needs to go, hoping every strap holds and every corner survives the trip.

This one's about that person. The one you never see and never think about until a board shows up cracked, or doesn't show up at all.

If you haven't watched it yet, here it is.
